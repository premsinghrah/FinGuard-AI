ERPNext Integration Pipeline

FinGuard AI operates in two operational configurations:

Mode 1: Embedded Custom Frappe App

Installed directly into your Frappe Bench. Triggers synchronous internal control checks on Journal Entry & Payment Entry submit hooks, plus scheduled hourly and daily batch scans.

Mode 2: Standalone Microservice / Simulation (Active)

Currently active. Pulls ledger data via ERPNext REST APIs or operates on the local simulation stream with full scikit-learn Isolation Forest, RapidFuzz, and composite scoring algorithms.
