Engine Highlights

Engineered Specifically for Enterprise Finance

FinGuard AI moves beyond primitive static rules by integrating automated machine learning with auditable internal control frameworks.

Unsupervised Isolation Forest

Trained directly on your company's historic general ledger data without requiring pre-labeled fraud datasets. Isolates multidimensional statistical outliers based on 14 engineered financial features.

10 Deterministic Audit Rules

Automates classic internal control checks: exact round amounts, off-hours creations (20:00–08:00), weekend entries, self-transfers, burst velocity spikes, zero balances, and missing narrations.

Fuzzy Duplicate Detection

Employs RapidFuzz token-sort similarity algorithms to intercept near-duplicate invoices, repeated vendor claims, and double payments across configurable date windows.

Feature Attribution & Deviation

No black boxes. Every flagged voucher details exact parameter divergence vs historical baselines (e.g. +64.8x amount deviation, off-hours timing, abnormal payment frequency).

Structured Audit Workflow

Assign vouchers to auditors, log investigation notes, escalate to the CFO, and capture structured false-positive feedback categories to refine future heuristics.

Deep Frappe/ERPNext Integration

Seamlessly hooks into `Journal Entry.on_submit` and `Payment Entry.on_submit` for synchronous screening, supported by hourly delta scans and daily model retraining.

How It Works

End-to-End Accounting Verification Pipeline

Every financial document submitted in ERPNext flows through a multi-tier risk screening architecture before reaching final general ledger commitment.

1

Frappe Document Hook

`on_submit` triggers `quick_scan` on Journal Entries and Payment Entries instantaneously.

2

Feature Vectorization

`FeatureEngineer` computes 14 numerical features (temporal, monetary, frequency, and account classes).

3

Tri-Core Scoring

Combines Isolation Forest ML (40%), Deterministic Rules (35%), and Fuzzy Duplicates (25%).

4

Audit Investigation

Generates `Financial Anomaly Alert` with plain-English diagnosis, attribution, and auditor assignment.
